# Privacy review

## Observers

Chain observers see published ledger fields. The app operator can know off-chain context. A person with local file access may read the lesson 17 plaintext teaching notebook.

## Public linkage

The public participant ID links that record’s commitment and reveal. The rating becomes public after a successful reveal. A scoped hash does not guarantee anonymity.

## Recovery

Losing the original salt prevents opening that commitment. The teaching notebook uses exclusive creation and restrictive file permissions; it is not encrypted and needs a deliberate backup/encryption design for sensitive use.

## Limits

The tests check this receipt allowlist and the known concept questions. They do not audit every log, prove anonymity, inspect provider metadata, or verify live network privacy.
